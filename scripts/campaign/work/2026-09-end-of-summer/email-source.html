Don't let fall start the same way summer ended
Gym in one place. Swim somewhere else. Pickleball wherever you can find a court. That's how a lot of South Bay summers actually go — three drives, three check-ins, and the feeling that you never really landed.
School's back. The calendar fills. And "we'll look at a club" quietly becomes next July's project. If that sentence sounds familiar, this window is for you.
South End Club pulls together elements of a Health Club, a Racquet Club, and a Country Club on one 7-acre campus in Torrance. Fitness, water, courts, and a place to sit down after — without stitching it together across town.
A gym that doesn't feel like a compromise. Five training spaces — a Matrix weight room, dedicated cardio, free weights and stretching, an outdoor gym, and a renovated women's-only gym — plus group classes included. On seven acres, you're not fighting for a rack the way you would at a packed chain gym. More on our Fitness page.
Pools you don't have to leave town for. An 82° lap and recreation pool with a real family zone, a beach-entry shallow pool for little kids, poolside grill, café, and an outdoor jacuzzi. The last warm stretch of the year is a hard time to still be a visitor at someone else's weekend. See our Pools.
Courts that stay on your calendar in October. Tennis, pickleball, padel, squash, racquetball, and POP tennis — a spread most facilities in the area simply don't have. Pickleball open play is all levels, including if you've never held a paddle. See the Open Play Schedule or our Pickleball page.
Add sauna, steam, and jacuzzi in both locker rooms, plus a member lounge for dinner and a drink after. One campus. That's the point — not another login, another parking lot, another "we'll figure it out next season."
