# email-templates

## Layman's terms

Email templates for transactional emails. Thank-you emails, confirmations. Used when the system sends automated emails (e.g. tour referral thank-you).

## Medium understanding

HTML email templates. tour-referral-thank-you.html for referral confirmation. May be sent via Resend or CRM. Not web pages—email body content.

## Advanced

- **Files:** tour-referral-thank-you.html
- **Usage:** Resend, Engage Pro, or other email service
- **Scope:** Transactional email content
