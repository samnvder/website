Reference for league structure, schedule, fees, and rules. **Monday–Wednesday** session blocks are **10:00 AM – 12:00 PM**. **Open (5.0+)** is the Friday 7:00 PM session.

---

- The fee is **due in full** at registration or by the **established deadline**.
- **$250** — team includes **any non-member**.
- **$200** — team is **all members**.
- **$75** — solo player, non-member.
- **$50** — solo player, member.

## League play structure

- **Round robin**, **doubles** format.
- Each team plays **two** opponents per week (**six games total** per league day), **one league day per week per division**.
- Teams may **stay together** or **swap players after each game** within a match (**three games per match**).
- **Results** are available online.
- Games use a **2-hour court block** per game session.
- **All games** count toward **standings** and **playoff seeding**.

---

## Roster requirements

- Players must be **18+**, or have **parent/guardian consent** and **signed liability** on file.
- A player may **roster on only one team**.
- Player **skill level** must fall within the **division range**.
- **Roster size:** min **4** / max **6** — recommend at least **4** rostered players.
- **Co-ed teams do not require two men or two women for any game. Co-ed simply allows teams to have both men and women; whether a game is played man/man, man/woman, or woman/woman is up to the team.**

---

## Team paperwork & lineups

- **Roster changes** are allowed through the **end of week 4**.
- Players must be **on the team roster** to play. Non-rostered players’ games are treated as a **forfeit** for the affected games.
- **Weekly matches:** captains enter the team **lineup online 30 minutes before** play.
- **Scorecards** are printed after lineups are entered.

---

## Match rules (in-game)

- Games to **11**, **win by 1**.
  - **Game 1:** home **serves**; away **selects side**.
  - **Game 2:** away **serves**; home **selects side**.
  - **Game 3:** home **serves**; away **selects side**; **switch at 6**.
- **One time-out** per set — **1 minute** (optional).
- **No stalling** for long strategy huddles — keep play moving.
- The league is **not sanctioned** and is **self-officiated**. If play issues persist or there are **disputed illegal serves**, contact the **league coordinator**; they will observe and decide next steps.
- For anything unclear, play follows the **USA Pickleball Official Rulebook:**  
  [USAP-Official-Rulebook.pdf](https://usapickleball.org/docs/rules/USAP-Official-Rulebook.pdf)

---

## Officiating & house rules

- **USA Pickleball Official Rules** are the baseline for how situations are read.
- The South End league is a **non-sanctioned** program; **non-officiated** practices apply.
- The club **may adjust rules** to fit the program when needed.
- **Ethical line calling** is required; on close calls, default to **in**.

---

## Playoffs

- **Eligibility:** must have played at least **two weeks** in the **regular season**.
- **Seeding / rankings** use **wins** during the season.
- **Tiebreakers (order):** (1) **head-to-head**, (2) **point differential**.

---

## Divisions, movement & eligibility

- Players clearly **above or below the division** may be **removed mid-season** or have **weekly game limits** applied.
- **Relegation and qualifiers** may apply. Teams are **notified if** they must play **qualifiers** the next season. Divisions are **not** an automatic up/down from season results alone.

---

## Equipment, balls & clothing

- Players supply **own paddles** (see **Paddle compliance** below).
- The league provides **game balls** — **return** after play.
- The league does **not** provide **warm-up balls**.
- **Ball in use:** **Franklin X-40** — **optic** color for play.

### Paddle compliance

- Paddles must be **USAP approved** and on the **current official list**.
- **Knockoff** and **delisted** paddles are **not** allowed.

---

## Etiquette

- **Good sportsmanship** at all times.
- **Ethical, respectful** play is required.
