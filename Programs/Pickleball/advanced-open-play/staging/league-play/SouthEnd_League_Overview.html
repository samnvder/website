Program rules

League Play Rules & Schedule

South End Pickleball League uses structured doubles match play, division-based scheduling, online results, standings, and playoff seeding.

Register

League fees

  • The fee is due in full at registration or by the established deadline.
  • $250 for teams with any non-member.
  • $200 for teams with all members.
  • $75 for solo non-member registration.
  • $50 for solo member registration.

League play structure

  • 6-week league with a one week break followed by playoffs.
  • Round robin doubles format.
  • Each team plays two opponents per week: six games total per league day, one league day per week per division.
  • Teams may stay together or swap players after each game within a match.
  • Results are available online.
  • Games use a 2-hour court block per session.
  • All games count toward standings and playoff seeding.

Roster requirements

  • Players must be 18+, or have parent/guardian consent and signed liability on file.
  • A player may roster on only one team.
  • Player skill level must fall within the division range.
  • Roster size: minimum 4, maximum 6.
  • Co-ed teams do not require two men or two women for any game. Co-ed simply allows teams to have both men and women; whether a game is played man/man, man/woman, or woman/woman is up to the team.

Team paperwork & lineups

  • Roster changes are allowed through the end of week 4.
  • Players must be on the team roster to play. Non-rostered players' games are forfeits.
  • Captains enter lineups online 30 minutes before play.
  • Scorecards are printed after lineups are entered.

Match rules

  • Games to 11, win by 1.
  • Game 1: home serves; away selects side.
  • Game 2: away serves; home selects side.
  • Game 3: home serves; away selects side; switch at 6.
  • One optional 1-minute time-out per set.
  • No stalling for long strategy huddles. Keep play moving.
  • The league is non-sanctioned and self-officiated.
  • For unclear situations, play follows the USA Pickleball Official Rulebook.

Playoffs

  • Players must have played at least two regular-season weeks to be eligible.
  • Seeding uses wins during the season.
  • Tiebreakers: head-to-head, then point differential.

Equipment & etiquette

  • Players supply their own paddles.
  • Paddles must be USAP approved and on the current official list.
  • The league provides Franklin X-40 optic game balls; return after play.
  • Good sportsmanship and ethical line calling are required.
