Experience South End Racquet & Health Club

South End is an Elite Athletic Club Haven, established in 1978, nestled amidst seven acres of beautifully manicured grounds in Torrance, California. This premier destination offers a comprehensive array of health/fitness facilities, racquet sports, a private social lounge, and much more, ecompassed within a family-friently, urban resort atmosphere.
