Partnerships That Work

What Are Corporate Memberships?

Discounted Rates Flexible Plans Premium Access

Corporate memberships are partnerships between local businesses and South End Club that offer exclusive membership benefits for employees. Companies can provide their teams with discounted access to our premium fitness, recreation, dining/social, and other wellness facilities—all designed to promote energy, productivity, and morale at work and beyond.

Program Features

Program Highlights

Employer Subsidies Multiple Facilities Wellness Classes

Our corporate program offers discounted employee membership rates with flexible employer subsidy levels. Members gain access to multiple gym facilities, pools, racquet sports, sauna/steam/spa, and much more. Optional wellness group exercise classes, social events and networking opportunities—all within a private and family-oriented club environment.
