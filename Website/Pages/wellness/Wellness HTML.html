Wellness Is Part of the Journey

Beyond the workout lies recovery — an essential part of every fitness journey. Our wellness spaces offer heat therapy, steam, and hydrotherapy to help you relax, restore, and prepare for whatever comes next. Whether you're easing sore muscles after training or simply taking time to unwind, these amenities make every visit restorative.

Heat Therapy

Dry Sauna

Deep Heat Detoxification Muscle Recovery

Heat. Relax. Recover.

Step into our dry sauna and let the enveloping warmth work its magic. The high-temperature, low-humidity environment penetrates deep into your muscles, promoting relaxation and helping release tension built up during your workout or daily life.

Perfect for post-exercise recovery, our sauna supports circulation, aids in detoxification through perspiration, and provides a quiet space to decompress. Many members find it's the ideal way to end their club visit.

Deep heat therapy to soothe muscles
Supports circulation and detoxification
Perfect after workouts or cardio
Respiratory Wellness

Steam Room

Moist Heat Skin Care Relaxation

Moist Heat for Mind & Body

Our steam room offers a distinctly different experience from the dry sauna. The warm, humid environment creates a soothing atmosphere where moisture envelops you, helping to relax muscles, open airways, and leave your skin feeling refreshed.

The gentle moist heat is especially comforting for those seeking respiratory relief or simply a calm moment in a humid, warm space. It's an excellent complement to any workout routine or a standalone wellness ritual.

Helps clear airways and ease tension
Hydrates skin and promotes circulation
Great for post-exercise cooldown
Hydrotherapy

Indoor Jacuzzis

Warm Jets Stress Relief All Locker Rooms

Warm Waters for Calm & Comfort

Sink into our indoor jacuzzis and let the warm, jetted water wash away the stress of the day. The soothing buoyancy combined with massaging water jets creates an ideal environment for relaxing tired muscles and finding a moment of peace.

Available in both men's and women's locker rooms, our jacuzzis provide hydrotherapy benefits that enhance recovery and promote a sense of well-being. It's a wonderful way to transition from an active workout to complete relaxation.

Hydrotherapy to relax tired muscles
Warm water jets for stress relief
Available in men's & women's locker rooms
