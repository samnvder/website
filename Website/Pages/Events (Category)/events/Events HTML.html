The Banquet Hall

A Hidden Jewel in the South Bay

Step inside Banquets by South End, the South Bay's most exclusive hidden jewel for elevated celebrations and signature event experiences.

The Banquet Hall at South End combines elegant design with complete event flexibility, making it one of the most versatile private event venues in the South Bay. This beautifully renovated 4,000 sq. ft. space features sculpted ceilings, customizable LED lighting, a spacious dance floor, and a fully equipped bar — giving you everything you need to create a seamless, memorable experience for your guests.

From weddings and receptions to milestone celebrations and corporate gatherings, the hall adapts effortlessly to any theme or décor style. Our in-house catering team offers a curated menu with customizable options, while our event specialists handle setup, coordination, and day-of support to ensure every detail is executed flawlessly.

Whether you're planning an intimate celebration or a large-scale production, South End's Banquet Hall delivers a sophisticated atmosphere, professional service, and a turnkey event experience that helps your vision come to life with ease.

The Lounge Events

From Casual to Polished

The Lounge at South End is built for everything from casual to polished private events — flooded with natural light by day and a vibrant glow by night.

Looking to make a statement? Have your next event in the Lounge! The Lounge can accommodate any form of cocktail style party or petite intimate sit down dining experience, with an extensive & gourmet food catalogue that caters to all individuals alike.
