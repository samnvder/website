Pickleball • 9 Courts

The South Bay's Premier Pickleball Destination

9 Professional Courts 3 Hybrid Paddle Courts Social & Competitive

Pickleball is one of the fastest-growing sports in America, and South End boasts some of the nicest courts in the South Bay. Blending the best of tennis, badminton, and table tennis, pickleball offers fast-paced rallies, strategy, and fun for all ages and experience levels. Our nine professionally maintained courts, including three hybrid paddle courts, are set up for both social and competitive play.

Padel • 2 Courts

Enclosed Court Padel Experience

Enclosed Outdoor Courts Turf Surface Fast-Paced Action

Padel is played on an enclosed outdoor court with turf. Scoring is the same as tennis, and the balls are similar, but the walls are in play – creating a fast, engaging game that's easy to learn and hard to stop playing.

POP (Paddle) Tennis • 3 Hybrid Courts

Scaled-Down Tennis Innovation

Shorter Courts Solid Paddles Lower-Compression Balls

POP Tennis is a scaled-down version of regular tennis played on shorter courts with solid paddles and lower-compression balls. The smaller court encourages quick reactions, fast rallies, and exciting doubles play – a fun twist for tennis players and a great entry point for beginners.

Squash • 2 Courts

Premier Squash Courts in the South Bay

Air-Conditioned Professional Grade Only Courts in 20-Mile Radius

Squash is played in a four-walled indoor court with a small rubber ball. South End features two air-conditioned squash courts — the only courts within a 20-mile radius.
